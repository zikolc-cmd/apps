Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026. This policy applies to the iOS game Elixa: Potion Sort Puzzle (“Elixa”, the “app”), published by Louka Morici, an independent developer based in Belgium (the “publisher”, “I”). Contact: zikolouka@gmail.com.

1. No account, no personal data asked

Elixa has no account and no login. I never ask for your name, email, phone number or address. Your game progress (levels, stars, drops, settings) is stored only on your device. It is not sent to me.

2. Advertising: Google AdMob

The free version shows ads provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC). To deliver and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

  • device identifiers (the Identifier for Vendors and, only if you allow it through Apple's tracking prompt, the advertising identifier IDFA);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • ad interactions and product interactions (for example, which ad was shown or tapped);
  • diagnostics (crash and performance data related to ads).

These data are used by Google for showing ads, frequency capping, fraud prevention and ad measurement. Google acts under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. I do not receive these data in a form that identifies you.

3. Your consent (GDPR) and tracking (ATT)

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Elixa shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before any ad is requested. You can accept, refuse or choose purposes, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy > Ad choices inside the app. If you refuse, you may still receive non-personalized ads. The current version does not ask for Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission and does not access the IDFA; if a future version asks, you will always be free to refuse, and the game stays fully playable.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases (No Ads, drop packs, Starter Pack) are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. I never see your payment details. The app only receives a confirmation from Apple that a purchase is valid, to deliver the content.

5. Notifications

The optional daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. It is off by default and can be turned off in the app settings or in iOS settings. No data is sent.

6. Data retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies (see the links above).

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, erase and restrict the processing of your data, to object to it, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Since I do not hold personal data about you, the simplest way to erase everything stored by the app is to delete Elixa from your device. For data held by Google, you can use My Ad Center, reset your device's advertising identifier in iOS settings, or contact Google. You may also write to me at zikolouka@gmail.com, and you can lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

8. Children

Elixa is a puzzle game suitable for all ages, but it is not specifically directed at children. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ad requests are not marked as child-directed; parents can use Apple's Screen Time controls to limit purchases.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
