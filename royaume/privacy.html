ENGLISH

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains what data is processed when you play Goldcrest (iOS app, bundle ID com.loukamorici.royaume). The app is published by Louka Morici, an independent developer based in Belgium (the "publisher"). Contact: zikolouka@gmail.com.

1. The short version

  • No account, no login, no personal profile. The publisher does not run any server and receives no personal data.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your iPhone.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google may process limited data to show and measure ads, only after you have answered the consent form.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. The publisher never sees your payment details.

2. Data stored on your device

Your game save (gold, buildings, progress, settings, purchase receipts IDs) is stored locally on your device (app storage). It is not sent to the publisher. It is deleted when you delete the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows optional rewarded videos and occasional interstitial ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. To deliver, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • device identifiers (such as the identifier for vendors, and the advertising identifier only if you allow it in iOS);
  • approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • usage data: app launches, ad interactions (impressions, taps), advertising data;
  • diagnostic data: crash and performance information related to ads.

This data is not linked to your identity by the publisher. Google acts under its own policies: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

4. Consent (GDPR / UMP) and tracking (ATT)

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a Google consent form (User Messaging Platform) is shown before any ad is requested. You can accept, refuse or customize. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings (gear icon) → Ad Choices. If you refuse, you may still see non-personalized ads.

The app does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). Without that permission, the advertising identifier is not available to the app or to Google. You can also limit ad tracking in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

5. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The publisher only receives an anonymous confirmation that a product was bought, which is used to unlock it on your device. Refunds are handled by Apple.

6. Notifications

Optional local reminders (stewards finished, free chest, daily gift) are scheduled on your iPhone only. Nothing is sent through a server. You can turn them off in the game settings or in iOS Settings.

7. Retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Data processed by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies.

8. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict and object to the processing of your data, and to data portability. As the publisher holds no personal data about you, the simplest way to erase your game data is to delete the app. For data processed by Google, use the Ad Choices form or Google's tools (myaccount.google.com). For any question or request, write to zikolouka@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

9. Children

Goldcrest is a general-audience game and is not directed specifically at children. The publisher does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Parents can contact us at any time.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date at the top will change. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.

Tu peux modifier ton choix à tout moment : Réglages (roue dentée) → Choix publicitaires. L'app ne demande pas l'autorisation de suivi (ATT) : l'identifiant publicitaire n'est donc pas utilisé.

Tu disposes des droits d'accès, de rectification, d'effacement, de limitation, d'opposition et de portabilité. Pour effacer tes données de jeu, supprime l'app. Pour toute demande : zikolouka@gmail.com. Tu peux aussi introduire une plainte auprès de l'Autorité de protection des données (autoriteprotectiondonnees.be).

Enfants
