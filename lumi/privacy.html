Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026. This policy explains how the iPhone app Glowmood (bundle ID com.loukamorici.lumi) handles data. Publisher: Louka Morici, independent developer, Belgium. Contact: zikolouka@gmail.com.

1. Your journal stays on your iPhone

Glowmood has no account and no login. Your moods, emotions, intensity, factors, notes, gratitude entries, letters, settings and your companion's progress are stored only on your device (in the app's private storage). They are never sent to the developer or to any server. The developer cannot see them.

2. Data collected by Google AdMob (optional rewarded videos)

Glowmood only shows ads when you choose to watch a short rewarded video for bonus stars. These ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC). To serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and fix crashes, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process:

  • device identifiers (the vendor identifier; the advertising identifier IDFA only if you allow tracking in iOS),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • ad interactions and product interaction data (for example, that an ad was shown or completed),
  • diagnostic data (crash and performance data).

This data is not linked to your journal and the developer does not receive your journal contents. Google's own policy applies: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

3. Consent (GDPR) and tracking (ATT)

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, a Google consent form (UMP) is shown before any ad is requested. You can accept or refuse personalized ads, and change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad choices (GDPR) inside the app. The current version of Glowmood does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission and does not access the IDFA; if a future version does, iOS will ask you first and you can refuse. With Glowmood+ you never need to watch ads.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases (Glowmood+ and star packs) are processed by Apple through your Apple Account. The developer never receives your name, card details or payment information; the app only receives a confirmation from Apple that a purchase is valid.

5. Notifications

The optional evening reminder is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone. No push server is used.

6. Legal basis and retention

Local journal data: kept on your device until you delete it (Settings > Delete my data) or delete the app. Advertising data: processed by Google on the basis of your consent (or legitimate interest for non-personalized ads, fraud prevention and security), and kept according to Google's retention periods. E-mails you send to the developer are kept only as long as needed to answer you, and at most 2 years.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, the right to data portability and the right to withdraw consent at any time. Since the developer holds no journal data, you can erase everything yourself in the app. For any request, write to zikolouka@gmail.com; for data processed by Google you can also use My Ad Center. You may lodge a complaint with the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be) or the authority of your country.

8. Deleting your data

In the app: Settings > Delete my data erases your journal and companion from the device. Deleting the app also removes all local data. Glowmood+ can always be restored from your Apple Account.

9. Children

Glowmood is a general-audience wellbeing app and is not designed specifically for children. If you are under the age of digital consent in your country (13 to 16 depending on the country), please use Glowmood with a parent or guardian's permission. The developer does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

10. Health notice

Glowmood is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. It does not replace a doctor or mental health professional.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
