ENGLISH

Last updated: 1 November 2026. This policy explains what data the iPhone game Pompon Puffrise (the "App") uses. Publisher: Louka Morici, independent developer, Belgium. Contact: zikolouka@gmail.com.

1. No account, game data stays on your device

The App has no account, no login and no server of its own. Your progress (stars, cosmetics, levels, settings) is stored only on your iPhone. The publisher never receives it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC). To deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect: a device or advertising identifier, your approximate location derived from your IP address, interactions with ads and the App, and diagnostic data (crashes, performance). This data is used for third-party advertising and analytics. It is not linked to your identity by the publisher. iOS asks whether you allow tracking (App Tracking Transparency): only if you allow it may Google use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to personalize ads. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. See how Google uses data from partner apps.

3. Consent (GDPR / UMP)

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the App first displays Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform). Ads are requested only as allowed by your choice. You can change your choice at any time from the App's settings or by reinstalling the App. Rewarded videos are always optional.

4. Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple. The publisher never sees your name, e-mail or payment details; the App only receives a confirmation from Apple that a product was bought.

5. Retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete the App. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies (advertising identifiers can be reset in iOS settings).

6. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to access, rectify, erase and object to the processing of your data, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Belgium: the Data Protection Authority, dataprotectionauthority.be). To erase all game data, delete the App. For any request, write to zikolouka@gmail.com; for data held by Google, you can also use Google's privacy tools.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from them. Ads are not personalized when a user has not given consent.

8. Changes

This policy may be updated; the date above will change. Legal basis: consent (advertising) and legitimate interest (security and fraud prevention).

Pas de compte, données de jeu sur ton appareil

Les vidéos récompensées sont toujours facultatives.

Achats

Les achats sont traités par Apple. L'éditeur ne voit ni ton nom, ni ton e-mail, ni tes données de paiement.

Durée, droits, effacement

Les données de jeu restent sur l'appareil jusqu'à la suppression de l'app. Tu disposes des droits d'accès, de rectification, d'effacement et d'opposition (RGPD) et peux saisir l'Autorité de protection des données (Belgique). Pour tout effacer : supprime l'app. Pour toute demande : zikolouka@gmail.com.

Enfants
