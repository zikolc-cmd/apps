Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Slide anywhere to steer Pompon. Tap to dive onto a cloud; land near its center for a Perfect bounce.

Are chests ever sold?

No. Cloud chests are always free and can never be bought.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Shop or Settings › Restore Purchases, with the same Apple Account as for the original purchase.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on your iPhone only. Deleting the App deletes your progress.

How do I delete my data or change ad consent?

Delete the App to erase all game data. More in the Privacy Policy.

A purchase did not arrive / I want a refund

Try Restore Purchases first. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
